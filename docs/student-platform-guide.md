# YooBees Student Platform Guide

## 1) What is YooBees?
YooBees is your student portal for attendance, profile management, and class support resources.

Use it to:
- Mark attendance during active class checkpoints.
- Track your attendance history.
- Keep your student profile updated.
- Access MBI802 resources (if your intake/subjects include MBI802).

---

## 2) First-time setup

### Step 1: Sign in
- Open the YooBees login page.
- Sign in with your student account.

### Step 2: Select your intake
On first login, the platform prompts you to choose your intake:
- **2511** → subjects include **MBI804**
- **2604** → subjects include **MBI800** and **MBI802**

### Step 3: Upload your profile photo
You will be prompted to upload a profile photo. This helps lecturers and classmates identify you.

---

## 3) Main student menu
After login, use the sidebar to open:
- **Dashboard**
- **My Profile**
- **Attendance**
- **My History**
- **MBI802 Resources** (only shown when eligible)

---

## 4) Dashboard
The Dashboard gives a quick overview of your student experience, including profile-related highlights and batchmate information.

Use it to:
- Confirm your account/profile is recognized.
- View student community highlights.

---

## 5) My Profile (important)
Keep your profile complete and accurate.

Recommended fields to complete:
- Full name
- Student ID
- Campus and section
- Intake
- Course
- Home country and hometown pin on map
- Work background and educational background
- Special learning/support needs

Why this matters:
- Attendance submission can require profile details (especially campus and section).
- Accurate profile data helps lecturers support you better.

---

## 6) Attendance
The Attendance page is where you:
- Submit attendance during active checkpoints.
- Check current attendance windows.
- Submit absence notices when needed.

Tips:
- Submit as early as possible during active windows.
- Double-check your profile first if submission is blocked.

---

## 7) Quick Attend link (`/attend/<code>`)
Sometimes lecturers share a quick attendance code/link.

How to use it:
1. Open the shared attendance link.
2. Sign in as a **student** if prompted.
3. Confirm the session/checkpoint details.
4. Submit attendance before the countdown expires.

Important behavior:
- A checkpoint only works while active and not expired.
- Duplicate submissions are blocked.
- If your campus/section is missing, complete **My Profile** first.

---

## 8) My History
Use **My History** to review:
- Past attendance submissions
- Checkpoint-level records
- Submitted absence notices and their status

This is useful when you need proof of submission.

---

## 9) MBI802 Resources
If you are enrolled in **MBI802**, this page provides extra learning resources (such as setup/tutorial links shared by lecturers).

If you do not see this page, your current subject list likely does not include MBI802.

---

## 10) Common issues and fixes

### “I can’t submit attendance.”
- Check that the checkpoint is still active.
- Check your internet connection.
- Open **My Profile** and ensure campus + section are filled.

### “I can’t see MBI802 resources.”
- Verify your intake/subjects in your student profile.

### “I submitted, but I’m unsure it worked.”
- Check **My History** for your latest submission.

---

## 11) Good student practices
- Keep your profile updated each term.
- Submit attendance immediately when a checkpoint opens.
- Save screenshots if you encounter errors.
- Review **My History** regularly.

---

## 12) Quick checklist before class
- [ ] I can sign in.
- [ ] My profile includes campus and section.
- [ ] I know where Attendance and Quick Attend are.
- [ ] I can open My History to verify submissions.
