YooBees Platform Has Been Discontinued

YooBees platform was developed voluntarily as a living example project for MBI800 Strategic Information Systems to make blended learning more engaging and interactive for students. However, as per instructions from senior faculty, the YooBees platform is no longer permitted for use. Respecting their decision, all future teaching and learning activities will now continue solely through Blackboard using institutionally approved content and systems. We sincerely apologise for any inconvenience this may cause and deeply appreciate your understanding and support.
