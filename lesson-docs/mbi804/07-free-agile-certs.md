## 1. Purpose & learning objectives

A curated, non-graded resource list recommending four free Agile/Scrum credentials to complement the MBI804 Agile Scrum coursework (the deck and quiz documented in `05-agile-scrum-deck.md` and `06-agile-scrum-knowledge-check.md`). Framed explicitly as career-development supplementary material rather than assessed course content, closing with a direct, personally-signed call-to-action from the lecturer encouraging students to post their earned credential on LinkedIn and tag him.

## 3. UI & interaction design

A 2-column responsive card grid (`md:grid-cols-2`) for the four certifications — one column fewer than the sibling MySQL-certs list's 3-column grid, reflecting the shorter list. Green/emerald accent palette throughout (`#059669` family) matching the MBI804 course's assigned brand color, distinct from MBI802's purple/violet cert-list palette. A scoped `<style>` block defines a set of `fac-`-prefixed keyframe animations (float, glow, shimmer, pop-in, twinkle, ping-slow, slide-up) applied to the LinkedIn CTA panel's decorative elements (floating emoji, twinkling star glyphs, a pulsing "online" status dot next to the lecturer's name, a shimmering gradient text effect on the "Share Your Achievement!" headline, and a shimmering button background) — purely decorative, no functional state.

## 4. Component & state architecture

`FreeAgileCertsLesson` is a stateless functional component — no `useState`, no `useEffect`, no data fetching. The `certs` array (4 objects: `badge`, `badgeBg`, `badgeColor`, `title`, `tag`, `tagColor`, `description`, `linkLabel`, `href`, `cardBg`, `borderColor`, `accentColor`) and the `whyLinkedIn` array (4 objects: `icon`, `title`, `desc`) are both defined as local `const`s inside the component body and mapped directly to JSX — this means they're re-created on every render (no `useMemo`), which is harmless here since the component has no re-render-triggering state of its own. All links are plain `<a target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">` tags with no click tracking or analytics instrumentation found in source.

## 5. Rebuild notes

- This is one of the smallest and simplest lessons in the platform in architectural terms (no state, no backend, no gating logic of its own) — the bulk of the "content" to preserve in a rebuild is the exact cert copy and links above, not any interaction design.
- Unlike its sibling MBI802 "Free MySQL/SQL/Database Design Certifications" list — which was later promoted to a standalone public page at `/sql-certifications` with its own `SQLCertificationsPage.tsx` + `SQLCertificationsLesson.tsx` — this MBI804 equivalent remains gated-only and embedded inline in `CourseResources.tsx` rather than extracted to its own component file. If MBI804 content depth is ever brought up to par with MBI802/MBI800 (a gap explicitly noted across this subject's lesson docs), promoting this to a public page mirroring `SQLCertificationsPage`'s structure would be a natural next step — and there is already a near-identical public page for a related MBI804 topic, `02-jira-agile-certifications.md` in this same course (`JiraCertificationsPage`/`JiraCertificationsLesson`), which significantly overlaps in subject matter with certification #4 here (Atlassian University — Agile & Jira Fundamentals appears in both lists) — worth reconciling/deduplicating in any future consolidation.
- The four external links and the LinkedIn profile URL were not live-verified as part of this documentation pass — standard risk for any resource-list lesson on this platform; flagged the same way in the sibling MBI802 cert-list doc.
- The disclaimer text says "This course has no affiliation..." rather than naming MBI804 specifically, suggesting it may have been copy-pasted from the MBI802 version without full adaptation — worth tightening in a rewrite, though not a functional issue.
