Step 1 of 2 · Describe a real conflict you've experienced

Work, university, a team project, family — anything real. A strong write-up covers all four of these: (1) the background and the people involved (no real names), (2) what triggered the conflict, (3) how each side actually responded, and (4) how it ended, or where it stands now. Minimum 400 characters — a classmate has to be able to analyze this without asking you anything.
