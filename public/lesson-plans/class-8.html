Instructor Notes
📝 Pedagogical Notes for the Instructor
  • Concept map opening: The grand review concept map should be student-generated — project a blank template and ask students to suggest connections. Instructor facilitates rather than lectures.
  • SQL Practice Lab: Students who completed lab tasks in earlier classes may extend their scenario: add a second table, create a JOIN query, or normalise their schema to 3NF.
  • Assessment environment: Clearly communicate that the DBMS Knowledge Check is individual work. TAs should be positioned around the room to monitor without creating anxiety.
  • Results sensitivity: Students who do not pass (below 60%) should be offered a follow-up consultation — do not call out scores publicly. Platform records scores privately for instructor review.
  • Reflection quality: Encourage genuine reflection over surface-level responses. Model an example: "The concept that surprised me most was denormalisation — I assumed more normalisation was always better, but performance trade-offs in data warehousing changed my view."
  • Course closure: Acknowledge specific student achievements observed over the 8 classes — growth mindset reinforcement. Thank students for their engagement and wish them well in their database journey.
