## What is SISP?

**SISP** = process of identifying and prioritising IS investments so they support business strategy, decided *before* project budget is committed.

- Project planning asks *how* to build on time/budget.
- SISP asks *whether* the system should exist at all, and *why*.

## Why SISP is hard

- Systems **reshape strategy**, not just serve it (new data → new strategic options).
- Planning happens under **uncertainty + constrained resources** — technology outpaces planning cycles.
- Planning is a **human process** first — competing incentives, incomplete information, real disagreement.

::: tip
For every framework: ask what failure it prevents, and what breaks without it.
:::

## Five core competencies

1. Translate business objectives into IS requirements
2. Assess technology needs and implementation constraints
3. Risk management (built in, not bolted on)
4. Ethical / culturally responsive planning (e.g. Māori and Pasifika health IS — data sovereignty, cultural protocols, consent)
5. Learn from documented real-world case studies
