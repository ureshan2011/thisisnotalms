## Risk is ongoing, not one-time

**Risk register** = maintained record of risks + controls, reviewed on a schedule.

Monitor: has the rating changed? Are controls still effective?
Review: has likelihood/impact/control suitability/cost changed?

## Five frameworks

| Framework | Source | Focus | Fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NIST RMF** | NIST SP 800-37 Rev.2 | Cybersecurity built into SDLC | Public sector, critical infra, regulated |
| **ISO/IEC 27005** | ISO 27000 family | Supports ISO 27001 cert | Broad industry (finance, health, tech) |
| **COBIT** | ISACA | Enterprise IT governance | Aligning IT with business goals |
| **OCTAVE** | CMU SEI | Strategic asset/threat eval | Medium–large orgs, business impact analysis |
| **FAIR** | The Open Group | Quantitative — risk in $ | Boards / CISOs, cost-based decisions |

## NIST RMF — 6 steps

**Categorize → Select → Implement → Assess → Authorize → Monitor**

## OCTAVE — 3 steps

Identify assets & threats → evaluate vulnerabilities → develop protection strategy.

## Matching framework to context

- Need board budget justified in $ vs other capital projects → **FAIR**
- Pursuing ISO 27001 certification → **ISO/IEC 27005** (its risk companion)
- Government system needing formal authorisation before go-live → **NIST RMF**
- "What matters most if we lost it?" business-impact question → **OCTAVE**
- "Is IT governed/aligned with business goals?" → **COBIT**

::: tip
Frameworks compose — an org can run RMF + ISO cert + COBIT governance + FAIR reporting simultaneously, each for a different purpose.
:::
